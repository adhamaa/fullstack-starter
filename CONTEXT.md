# Radionic Homeopathy — Domain Glossary

This document defines domain terms only. It does not describe implementation.

## Remedy

A classical single homeopathic remedy — for example Arnica Montana, Belladonna, or Aconitum Napellus. Remedies are derived from animal, mineral, or vegetable sources and are described by their materia medica: symptoms, modalities, mental picture, and clinical indications.

## Formula

A composite remedy composed of one or more Remedies, often following radionic catalogues such as Bruce Copen's instrument formulas (e.g. "Migraine and Neuralgia"). A Formula has its own name, indication, and clinical profile distinct from any single component Remedy.

## Potency

The homeopathic dilution scale used when preparing or prescribing a remedy — for example 6C, 30C, 200C, 6X, or LM potencies. Potency describes how a substance was serially diluted and succussed. **Potency is a homeopathic pharmacy concept.**

## Rate

A numeric or symbolic code used on a radionic instrument to broadcast the vibrational pattern of a Remedy or Formula. Rates are catalogue-specific (e.g. `SC/3`, `G/21`, `C/15`) and are entered on the instrument dial or plate. **A Rate is not a Potency.**

## Rate Bank

A named catalogue of Rates with its own numbering scheme and source reference. Examples include Copen, Kelly, and Delawarr rate systems. Each bank assigns values independently; the same Remedy may have different Rates in different banks.

## Symptom Grade

In repertory and materia medica work, symptoms linked to a Remedy are often graded 1–4 to indicate how strongly that symptom indicates the remedy (1 = occasional, 4 = keynote).

## Modality

A condition that modifies a symptom — typically "Better from" or "Worse from" factors such as motion, heat, cold, pressure, or time of day. Modalities help differentiate remedies with similar symptom pictures.

## Materia Medica Source

A published reference (book, author, edition) that documents remedy provings and clinical observations. Sources are cited when linking remedies to page numbers or historical authority.

---

**Important:** Potency ≠ Rate. Potency belongs to homeopathic preparation and prescribing. Rate belongs to radionic broadcast catalogues. They must not be conflated in data models or user interfaces.
